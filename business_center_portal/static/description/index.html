Business Center Tenant Portal for Odoo

Business Center Portal gives the tenants of your business centre or co-working space their own self-service area at /my. Tenants book and cancel meeting rooms, follow their contracts and download their invoices — without emailing the front desk. Every page reads the same contracts, bookings and invoices your staff work with in the back office, so nothing has to be re-keyed or reconciled.

Important Note

This module does not work without the Business Center app (business_center). It is an add-on that adds the tenant portal on top of the Business Center back office: contracts, spaces, desks, meeting rooms and bookings all come from that app.

Please install the Business Center app first. Business Center Portal then installs automatically alongside it, or can be installed from the Apps menu. Uninstalling this module returns the database to back office only, with no leftovers.

Key Features of Business Center Portal

Self-Service Meeting Room Booking

Tenants pick a room and date, see free and busy slots on a live availability strip, and book against the contract they are named on. Other tenants' bookings show only as “Booked”, never with their reference.

Same Booking Rules as the Back Office

No past-dated bookings, no cancellation once a session has started, and times compared in the business centre's timezone. An extra charge already on an invoice can only be reversed by the centre.

Contracts at a Glance

Tenant corporate admins see their company's contracts, the offices and desks they lease and the contract dates — read-only, always in step with the back office.

Invoice History & Online Payment

Corporate admins view and download their company's posted invoices and credit notes, and pay online with Pay Now once a payment provider is enabled.

Two Tenant Roles

Tenant Employee and Tenant Corporate Admin, set on the contact and applied automatically when portal access is granted. A tenant contact granted access without a role gets the least privileged one.

Strict Tenant Separation

Each tenant sees only their own company's records, enforced by record rules at the database layer rather than by hiding menus. Portal users without a Business Center role keep Odoo's normal portal.

--- HOW IT WORKS ---

Giving Tenants Portal Access

Set the Business Center Role on the tenant's contact, then open Contacts → Action → Grant portal access.
The role is applied as soon as the login is created, and changing it later updates the user's access straight away.

RoleWhat they can do in the portal
Tenant EmployeeBook meeting rooms against the contracts they are named on, and see and cancel their own bookings. No access to contracts, other people's bookings or invoices.
Tenant Corporate AdminEverything an Employee can, plus the company's contracts, all of the company's bookings, and the company's invoice history with download and online payment.

Corporate Admin Dashboard

A Tenant Corporate Admin lands on My Account with tiles for Contracts, Bookings and Invoice History.

Employee Dashboard

A Tenant Employee sees only the Bookings tile — contracts and billing stay with the company's admin.

Meeting Room Bookings

The Bookings page lists active and past reservations with their room, date, time and status.
Employees see their own bookings; corporate admins see every booking made by their company.

New Booking

Choose the contract, meeting room and date. The availability strip shows the room's free and busy blocks for the day, so a tenant picks a time that is actually open.
Before confirming, the tenant sees the remaining free meeting-room hours and the cost summary for anything beyond them.

Cancelling a Booking

Upcoming bookings can be cancelled from the portal until the session starts. Once it has started, or once its extra charge is invoiced, only the business centre can reverse it.

Setup Checklist

StepWhere
1. Install Business CenterApps → Business Center. Required — this portal does not work without it.
2. Set the timezoneBusiness Center → Configuration → Settings. Booking times are checked in this timezone.
3. Assign rolesOpen the tenant's contact and set Business Center Role to Tenant Employee or Tenant Corporate Admin.
4. Grant portal accessContacts → Action → Grant portal access.
5. Enable online payment (optional)Invoicing → Configuration → Payment Providers — an online provider, or Wire Transfer with your bank details, to show Pay Now on invoices.
